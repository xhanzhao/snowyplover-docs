Animation tools for Grasshopper

SnowyPlover is an animation plugin for Grasshopper in Rhino. You move the camera, time the events, animate the model and export the frames to a video, all from one definition.

One number drives everything

Every SnowyPlover definition is built around a single number, t, that runs from 0 to 1 over the length of the animation. Each component turns t into something you can see: a camera position, a section plane, a colour. Drag the slider below to see how Event Timeline splits one t into two events, one for the camera and one for the section cut.

t   = slider 0.30 live
ev  = EventTimeline [t] events="camera: 0-0.6, section: 0.5-1"
nv  = GetCameraFromNamedviews [used]
key = AnimateKeyframes [used]
cam = ApplyCamera [used]
g   = param Geometry "Building"
cut = AnimateSectionCut [used]

t ~> ev.t
nv.camera -> key.cameras
ev.event1 ~> key.t
key.camera -> cam.camera
g -> cut.geometry
ev.event2 ~> cut.t

The orange wires carry t. The numbers on them change as you drag.

When the animation looks right, View Capture Driver takes over t. It steps t from 0 to 1 one frame at a time and saves each frame from the Rhino viewport, and FramesToVideo turns the frames into an MP4.

What is in the plugin

  • CameraKeyframes from Named Views, orbits, dolly moves, dolly zoom, swings to plan and elevation.
  • TimelineSplit t into eased events, preview in real time, and step through design options.
  • EffectSection cuts, growing curves and surfaces, arrows, moving and scaling objects.
  • DisplayFading colours, gradients, text on the viewport and live mesh outlines.
  • VideoSave frames, then make an MP4, MOV or GIF, or join several shots with transitions.
  • UtilityRandom layers, materials from layer colours, and helpers for sliders and domains.
